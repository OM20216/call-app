Live app

HR click-to-call

Click Call on your laptop — your phone rings, then the candidate is bridged in. Every call is stored with pickup time, hang-up time, talk duration and full history.

Open the call app (full server) → Open Pages app (synced) Deployment guide Source on GitHub

QR calling

Each candidate gets a QR. Scan it on any phone, tap Call, and the attempt is recorded instantly.

Call history

Status, local timestamp, pickup time, talk time and total talk time per contact — computed from every attempt.

Per-admin lists

Every recruiter sees only their own contacts and their own call counts.

HRMS sync

Candidates are pushed from the HRMS by crm_candidate_id; call stats flow back to the modal.

Two versions, now connected. The full server version (Twilio bridge, shared counts, HRMS sync) runs on Node — GitHub Pages cannot run it. The static version on this site now also syncs to the call server: QR → dial → measured talk time, and every call is shared across your devices and the Node dashboard.
